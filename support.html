FAQ

How do I learn to count? Open Learn → Card Counting for the Hi-Lo system, then in a game turn on Show Count (the eye button at the top) to watch the count change with every card. When you're comfortable, switch to Quiz mode to test yourself.
What does Pro unlock? A one-time purchase unlocks Quiz mode, the full basic strategy / pairs / insurance charts, and progress stats. The full game and the live count display are free. There is no subscription.
I bought Pro but it's not unlocked. Go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you purchased with. If it still doesn't appear, email us.
Is card counting legal? Yes — card counting is simple mental math and is completely legal to learn and practice. This app is a trainer with no real money. (Casinos may ask advantage players to leave, and phones aren't allowed at live tables — this is for practice at home.)
Why is it rated 17+? The app has simulated-gambling themes (a blackjack table and chips). There is no real money and nothing to win or lose but practice chips.
