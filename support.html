FAQ

How do I learn to count? Open Learn → Card Counting and swipe through the Hi-Lo lesson. Then, at the table, tap the eye button to turn on Show Count and watch the running count change with every card. When you're comfortable, switch to Quiz to test yourself.
What is free, and what does Pro unlock? Everything that teaches is free: the lessons, the live running count, Quiz mode, the coach, and the strategy chart. Free play includes 20 hands every 24 hours. The one-time Pro Unlock purchase removes that limit. There is no subscription.
I bought Pro but it's not unlocked. Go to Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with. If it still doesn't appear, email us.
Is this gambling? No. This is a practice trainer. Chips are virtual and have no cash value, there is no real-money gambling, and there is nothing to win. The app is not intended for use in a casino, and phones are not allowed at live tables.
Why is it rated 18+? The app simulates a blackjack table with chips, which the App Store rates as simulated gambling. There is no real money involved.
