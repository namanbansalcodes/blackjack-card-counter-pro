Privacy Policy

Hi-Lo Blackjack Trainer by Green Felt Games · effective September 15, 2026

Short version: the app collects no data about you. It is a single-player, offline practice trainer. There are no accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. We run no servers, so we never receive anything from the app.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (count mode, coach and haptics), quiz statistics, and the free-hands counter are saved only on your device, inside the app's private storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Data retention and deletion

Because nothing is sent to us, we hold no data about you and there is nothing to request or delete on our side. Data on your device is kept until you remove it. You can reset the game at any time in Settings → New Game, and deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. No consent is requested because no personal data is collected; there is nothing to revoke.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro Unlock purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Third parties

The app shares no data with any third party. Its only external service is Apple's App Store purchase system.

Children

The app is rated 18+ for simulated gambling themes and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and its effective date will be posted on this page.
